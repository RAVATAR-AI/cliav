What is an on-premise AI avatar for hospitals?

An on-premise AI avatar is a real-time, interactive virtual assistant that runs on your hospital’s own servers.
It handles the patient conversations that don’t need a clinician, and patient data stays under your control.

  • Real-time voice and video. A face, a voice, and natural turn taking. Voice only where video isn’t wanted. Runs in the browser, no app to install.

  • Any screen a patient meets. Kiosk in the waiting area, Holobox at the entrance, and web, mobile, or telephone with the on-premise option. One persona on every screen.

  • Private by design. Runs on your own servers, with access, AI models, and updates under your IT team’s control.

  • Answers only from your content. The avatar reads from forms and material your clinical team approved. Nothing is trained on patient data.

It doesn’t diagnose, interpret symptoms, triage, or prescribe.

AI patient intake before the visit

AI patient intake replaces the paper form with a conversation. An avatar on a kiosk, screen, or hologram talks with the patient in their own language, by voice or text, and collects what a hospital needs before care starts: who the patient is, why they are coming, and what the care team needs to know about their health.

It asks every patient the same questions in the same order, and follows up when an answer is unclear.

Questions from hospital review teams

Does Cliav need an internet connection?

Not in the air-gapped option: only the internal systems you choose. On-premise connects as your IT team allows.

Where do the models run?

On site. Open-weight models run on your own servers, so your hospital owns its intelligence.

Who controls access and updates?

Your IT team. Cliav runs on your own servers, so access, AI models, and updates stay under its control.

Who approves the clinical notes?

The doctor. A device in the room drafts the notes, and the doctor approves them.

Do patients know they’re talking to an AI?

Yes. Cliav tells patients it’s an AI, and an on-screen notice says so too.

How does a pilot work?

Pick one role and one location, load your approved content, run it for an agreed period, and review the analytics.

Does Cliav replace clinicians?

No. It handles routine conversations. It doesn’t diagnose, interpret symptoms, triage, or prescribe.
